⚡ News Flash
One Migration. Zero Drama. ₹50 Lakh Saved...
Big transformations rarely happen quietly—but this one did. The ICC migration was completed with minimal customer disruption while maintaining service levels above 85%. It has already delivered nearly ₹50 lakh in savings through May, creating a stronger, leaner foundation. While the migration succeeded, NPS briefly slipped into negative territory, impacting the Customer Experience Index.
... But What Did the Customers Say? 😇
Anubhav NPS stayed stable with a 2 point drop from Q4 to Q1 😮‍💨, although ICC climbed back to +1 in June and put us back in positive territory as we head into the next quarter. On the complaints front too, things stayed steady: complaints/1000 moved from 0.34 in Q4 to 0.24 in Q1. No drama here—and when it comes to complaints, boring is good. 😄

⚡ More News to Know
X-Sell PL Just Hit a New High 🚀
Cross-sell PL volumes raced past Q4 levels at 726 crores (ICC + OCC). The inbound team beat its run rate by ₹30 cr despite all the migration action in the background. But the surprise package was our three-month-old voice bot PIA, delivering ₹16 cr, growing 3X since launch. And 10% of that business was completely DIY by the customer. Not bad for someone who’s still learning to walk. 🤖
3 Lakhs and Counting 📱
The Piramal Mobile App just crossed a milestone we've never seen before—3.05 lakh Monthly Active Users, the highest in company history. More customers are tapping, tracking, and transacting than ever before. Safe to say, the app is having a moment. 🚀
📊 Let's talk digits
#231
Our WhatsApp bot was built for efficiency, but one customer insists on beginning every conversation with a respectful "जय ओम बन्ना सा री हुकम"—231 times and counting. 🙏 A lovely reminder that even as our channels go digital, our customers make sure the bots stay connected to tradition.
🚀 New Launches
VRMs Are Getting Smarter 📞
The VRM PL Prime campaign keeps evolving. One key insight: customers still need better clarity on applicable charges. Next up? Tracking post-call drawdowns, a standardized RM introduction, and birthday outreach to build stronger relationships. Small tweaks, smarter conversations, and a better CX ahead.
The ₹66 Cr Reasons to Stay 🏦
Retention had a solid Q1. Proactive top-ups delivered ₹23 Cr in disbursals against a ₹25 Cr target, while our broader retention initiatives added a positive delta of ₹66 Cr to the portfolio. Turns out, sometimes the best customer to win is the one you already have. 🙂
